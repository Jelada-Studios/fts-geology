# FT's Geology — Realistic Plate Tectonics, Landforms & Geophysics for Minecraft Forge 1.20.1

A living geology layer for Minecraft: real plate tectonics driving real landforms.

Most world-generation mods decide what a place looks like. **FT's Geology decides *why*.**

Instead of scattering volcanoes, springs, and quakes by arbitrary chance, FT's Geology runs an underlying plate tectonic simulation. Volcanoes, fault scarps, geyser basins, and earthquakes emerge from the mechanical and thermal interactions of drifting lithospheric plates.

---

## Key Highlights

- **Plate Tectonic Engine**: Distinct continental and oceanic plates with continuous drift vectors, forming rift valleys, continental collisions, transform faults, and subduction zones.
- **Volcanoes Shaped by Tectonic Chemistry**: Low-viscosity shield volcanoes over hotspots, explosive composite stratovolcanoes along subduction arcs, massive caldera collapses, and rift fissure eruptions.
- **Earthquakes with Early Warning**: Real wave physics with fast P-waves triggering seismograph alarms ~10s before destructive S-waves arrive.
- **True Ground Physics**: **Nothing is destroyed, but unsupported blocks fall.** Unsupported ground and structures settle like sand and survive intact; nothing is deleted. Both `quakesBreakBuilds` and `fallingIncludesBuilds` are configurable.
- **Living Geothermal Basins**: Evolving hot springs featuring 4 natural stages: nascent boiling pools, temperature-zoned microbial mats, travertine/sinter terraces with bubbling mud pots, and senescent fumarole chimneys venting steam with native sulfur deposits.
- **Field Instruments**: Geologist's Hammer (rock classes & stratigraphic sections in real meters), Fault Compass (Brunton compass for strike, drift velocity & strain), Drum Seismograph (S-P distance & Richter magnitude), and the in-game **Geological Field Guide** book.
- **Complete Localization**: Full English (`en_us`) and Turkish (`tr_tr`) translations for all blocks, items, messages, and the 18-page field guide.
- **Zero Hard Dependencies**: Runs standalone on Minecraft Forge 1.20.1. Stacks seamlessly on top of Terralith and Tectonic, and detects Flowing Fluids (finite water) when present.
